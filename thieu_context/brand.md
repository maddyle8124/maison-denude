## Value Proposition

- **Core:** Bespoke design & tailoring — made for the individual, not off-rack
- **Aesthetic:** Heritage-inspired × sultry × feminine power (Vietnamese tradition + contemporary luxury)
- **Provenance:** Made in Saigon — geographic marker + brand story
- **Target buyer:** Women wanting unique items; occasion/event dressing; expats and international clients

## Client Communication Style

**Maison Dénudé's team is non-technical.** When presenting or communicating:
- Never use technical terms (Supabase, Astro, Edge Functions, CDN, etc.)
- Never lead with "free hosting" or "zero operating cost" — sounds cheap for a premium brand; their trust comes from quality and outcomes, not cost savings
- Always translate tech into business outcomes: "Google tìm thấy nhanh hơn", "team nhận thông báo ngay", "không bỏ sót khách hàng"
- Keep explanations short and visual — they trust results, not process

## Brand Voice

- Confident, not loud
- Luxurious without coldness
- Deeply Vietnamese references, internationally readable execution
- Editorial (imagery and feeling, not specs)

## Brand Strengths

1. Strong premium áo dài & occasion wear positioning — consistently chosen for major events (WeChoice, Vạn Xuân, Lễ Hội Áo Dài)
2. Swimwear breakout signal — Linh An post shows expansion potential beyond áo dài into resort/swimwear
3. Bridal/ăn hỏi is high-value niche — Cô Em Trendy (9K) + The Planners data proves demand
4. Multi-tier KOL reach: singers, models, classical musicians, pageant titleholders

## Brand Weaknesses

1. Low UGC — almost all mentions from KOL/verified; no mass word-of-mouth yet
2. 4.4K followers despite ELLE, L'Officiel, Miss Universe coverage — awareness not converting to audience
3. Minimal video/Reels — mostly static photography
4. No international digital foothold
