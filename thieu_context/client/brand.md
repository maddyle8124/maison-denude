## Value Proposition

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Core offer** | Bespoke design & tailoring — made for the individual, not off-the-rack |
| **Aesthetic** | Heritage-inspired × sultry × feminine power — intersection of Vietnamese tradition and contemporary luxury |
| **Provenance** | Made in Saigon — both a geographic marker and a brand story |
| **Target buyer** | Women who want what no one else has; occasion/event dressing; expats and international clients drawn to Vietnamese designers |

## Brand Voice

- Confident, not loud
- Luxurious without being cold
- Deeply Vietnamese in reference, internationally readable in execution
- Editorial — speaks in imagery and feeling, not product specs

## Brand Strengths

1. **Strong positioning in premium áo dài & occasion wear** — consistently chosen for major events (WeChoice, Vạn Xuân, Lễ Hội Áo Dài)
2. **Swimwear breakout** — Linh An post signals potential beyond áo dài into resort/swimwear
3. **Bridal/ăn hỏi is a high-value niche** — Cô Em Trendy (9K) + The Planners data proves demand
4. **Multi-tier KOL reach** — spans singers, models, classical musicians, pageant titleholders

## Brand Weaknesses

1. **Low UGC** — almost all mentions from KOL/verified accounts; no mass word-of-mouth yet
2. **No international foothold** — chicken-and-egg problem for global expansion
3. **Underdeveloped video/Reels** — most content is still photography
4. **Followers low vs. editorial coverage** — 4.4K followers despite ELLE, L'Officiel, Miss Universe coverage

## International Potential

- Global trend toward artisanal & slow fashion (reaction to fast fashion)
- Southeast Asian designers gaining global attention
- Bespoke is a service that cannot be replicated online → drives physical bookings
- Chi Pu connection → potential China market activation (Xiaohongshu / Weibo)
