## What changed vs. the proposal

**1. Scope: Landing page → Full website (confirmed)**
The client (Chi) cancelled her contract with the previous landing page vendor mid-meeting — their work was slow, they never followed up, and charged 3M VND for a half-done page. She passed the full job to Thiệu + Maddy on the spot. The plan was always a full website; this just removes the ambiguity.

**2. Add-on B (Wishlist) — client is clearly sold on it**
Chi articulated the exact use case herself: clients scroll your whole archive (not seasonal), screenshot outfits, and show up at the showroom with a collection of screenshots. She wants a "pin / wishlist" feature so when they book, the team already sees what they're interested in — no back-and-forth. She said "nếu mà mắc quá thì thôi" (if too expensive, skip) but the intent is strong. **Price is not confirmed yet** — Thiệu said he'd need to recalculate.

**3. Keyword strategy expanded to 3 market segments**
Chi herself called this out: these are three separate battlefields, each needing its own keywords:
- **Áo dài** (traditional, heritage)
- **Bridal / áo dài cô dâu** (wedding segment)
- **Designer / occasion wear** — including `evening wear`, `evening dress` because international customers don't know what "áo dài" is, but they recognize it as something you can wear to a party

**4. International KOL list added to Maddy's scope**
After market selection, Chi wants a filtered shortlist of KOLs per market — not just follower count but engagement quality, content consistency, career longevity. She'll provide her criteria after the 3 target markets are confirmed. This is an explicit addition to the Playbook.

**5. Booking CTA confirmed: everything channels to Zalo + WhatsApp**
Facebook and Instagram are discovery/browsing platforms. All CTAs — from website, from Facebook auto-reply — should offer two paths: (1) fill the booking form, or (2) jump straight to Zalo/WhatsApp. The team confirmed this is the conversion funnel that actually works for their business.

**6. KOL photos go on the homepage — prominently**
Chi explicitly said: when someone opens the website, they should immediately see Chi Pu, Băng Di, Văn Mai Hương etc. in the outfits. Even international visitors who don't know those names respond to the image quality. This is not a subtle "as-featured-in" strip — it's hero content.

**7. Blog content: existing content from internal writers will be expanded**
Linh Đan has written brand content before. Hải Hà (Communication background, likes to write) will expand the existing material to hit the keyword and length requirements Thiệu sets. Client asked team not to rush this — content takes time.
