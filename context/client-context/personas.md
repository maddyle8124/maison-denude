# Maison Dénudé — End-Customer Personas

_Last updated: 2026-06-27. Early signals from the 27/6 call (MD team). Still a draft — a formal ICP is the #1 blocker (OQ-012); MD team to deliver served / aspired / classification-criteria draft ~6/28._

## Headline shifts (MD team, 27/6)

- **Buyer base is moving VN locals → Việt Kiều.** Clear shift toward diaspora — US, Australia, Canada (possibly expanding to Singapore, France, Germany).
- **Product mix is rebalancing.** Áo dài vs. sườn xám / occasion (đầm) wear is now **roughly 50/50** (before last year, áo dài was the majority).
- **Online presence is a felt need.** Foreign customers routinely ask *"Do you have a website?"* — a direct demand signal that motivated this build.
- **Acquisition = social + word of mouth.** Mostly social media + WOM via viral weddings; customers often can't name the source ("saw it somewhere online").
- **Maison = designer / bespoke / private appointment.** Not a mass brand. Buyers invest heavily and expect a personalized A-to-Z experience.

## Persona map

| Persona | Description | Priority |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Việt Kiều occasion/áo dài buyer | Overseas Vietnamese (US/AU/CA) wanting a one-of-a-kind áo dài or occasion piece — often timed to a trip home or a family event; expects online discovery + a way to engage before arriving | **Rising — primary focus** |
| Domestic premium occasion buyer | VN-local woman seeking a bespoke outfit for an important occasion (evening/đầm, sườn xám); discovered via social + viral-wedding WOM | High (current base) |
| Bridal / ăn hỏi buyer | High-value wedding/engagement occasion; the viral-wedding channel feeds this | High |
