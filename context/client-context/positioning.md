## Brand-naming philosophy (from founders — 27/6)

Maison Dénudé is **deliberately not an eponymous designer label.** Michelle: *"chị không muốn brand lấy tên của mình… nó là một team, không phải một cá nhân."* **"Maison" = House** — the reference set is *Maison Dior / Maison Valentino*, and the aspiration is *"một cái religion."* → Build the **House** as the character, not Chi-the-person. (Nuance: Chi's own keyword set still wants "+ designer" searches to evoke her — reconcile by growing the House brand while Chi remains its creative face, not its name.)

## Core differentiators

- **Made-to-measure designer service** is the core reason to choose Maison. Note (27/6): this does **not** require the client to come in person — Việt Kiều order bespoke online and ship; Michelle already serves them remotely. So "a reason to come directly" is one path, not the only one.
- **Heritage-inspired Vietnamese craftsmanship** at a moment when artisanal / slow fashion and Southeast Asian designers are gaining global attention.
- **Earned credibility** — real KOL and editorial association (vs. paid-only awareness).

## Why this has international potential

- Global shift back toward **artisanal & slow fashion** (a reaction to fast fashion).
- **Southeast Asian designers** are having a moment in global fashion.
- Bespoke creates a built-in reason for direct, high-trust client relationships.

## Brand-language rules (from founder — treat as law)

Chi has explicit, non-negotiable wording preferences (WhatsApp 6/11–6/12):

- **Never "high end"** — she finds it cheap-sounding ("nghe stupid"). Use **premium** or **upscale**.
- **"Bespoke" already implies luxury** — don't stack "luxury bespoke." **Scope note (post-OQ-011):** "bespoke" now strictly means the *design-from-scratch* path; the *existing-model → measurements* path is "made-to-measure," not "bespoke." Don't use flat "bespoke" as the blanket label for the whole house — use "made-to-measure designer house" and reserve "bespoke" for the full-custom route.
- **Not a "tailor"** — Maison Dénudé is a designer house, not a tailoring shop. Avoid "tailor" / "may đo" as the brand's own descriptor.
- Lean into **artisanal / heritage / hand-craft** vocabulary: hand embroidery, hand-guided embroidery, hand embellished, hand beading, embroidery art.
- **Custom-language (27/6):** use *custom dress / custom service / custom bridal / Asian bridal core* — but **avoid the "24-hour tailoring" association** (Maison's custom = investment, not fast consumption).
- **Target buyer (refined 27/6):** Việt-Kiều-led (gốc Việt / married to Vietnamese / gốc Hoa/Á) buying for a milestone (wedding, graduation, important event) — online-and-ship works; plus domestic premium occasion/bridal buyers.
