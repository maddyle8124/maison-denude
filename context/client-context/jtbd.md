## A. Maison-as-client JTBD (what they hired the engagement to do)

This is the yardstick for every deliverable and the proposal. Format: *when [situation] → I want [progress] → so [outcome].*

1. **Turn data into direction + action** (the core job)
   *When* I have a website/traffic but **can't read the numbers myself** → *I want* the team to read the data and tell me which way it's trending **and what to do** → *so* I can decide fast, especially around launches and pre-Tết.
   > Chi: *"Chị cần solution… cái gì là action của team."* · Michelle: *"Số liệu nhiều quá chị rối, chị không đọc được đâu."*
   **Emotional job attached: give me clarity, don't drown me in numbers.**

2. **Get an online presence to capture the Việt-Kiều pool**
   *When* foreign customers keep asking *"do you have a website?"* → *I want* a polished online storefront → *so* I capture/convert the Việt Kiều pool that is now the majority (they order + ship; they do NOT need to fly to Vietnam).

3. **Understand who the customer actually is** (the ICP job)
   *When* I'm unsure who the customer really is / where / why they buy → *I want* help shaping a correct ICP → *so* I don't pour resources in the wrong place. (This is why all research is gated on the ICP — see OQ-012.)

4. **Hold the designer-House position + find high-spend pools**
   *When* the áo dài market is being commoditized/price-cut and pulling cheaper buyers away → *I want* to hold the **premium designer-House** identity and find high-spend segments → *so* I'm not dragged into a price war.

5. **Learn from competitors correctly + not fall behind**
   *When* public data is scrapeable by anyone → *I want* to learn competitors' behavior/sources/keywords **in my own segment** and protect internal data → *so* a late entrant can't just "learn everything."
   > Chi: *"ông đi sau lấy dữ liệu là xong."*

**Social/relationship job (implicit but strong):** *"Understand my brand's specialness."* Friction spikes when competitors are mis-segmented or the brand name is misspelled. They did **not** hire the team to hear generic industry reports, nor to define the brand for them — they **co-produce insight** (team = data/execution engine; founders = market-knowledge holders).

### What the proposal must therefore prove
| Maison job | Proposal must answer |
|---|---|
| #1 Data → action | For each data output, **what decision it enables** (not just "what we can get") |
| #3 Correct ICP | A method to **validate the ICP** before scraping wide |
| #4 Position + spend | A **purchasing-power POV** + how to find high-spend pools (bank/watch = feasible; RE/intl-school parents = needs validation) |
| #5 Learn correctly | Competitor picks by **consideration set**, not audience-overlap |

## B. End-customer JTBD (why customers buy from Maison)

### Core job (3 layers)
- **Functional:** *"Have a one-of-a-kind, made-to-measure outfit for a major occasion — something I can't buy off-the-rack."*
  > Chi: designer brand = *"từ mẫu có sẵn chế cho khách, hoặc chế mẫu mới từ đầu — đi từ vải, đi từ thiết kế, theo private appointment chứ không walk-in."*
- **Emotional:** *"Feel special, confident, striking and fully myself in a key moment — not off-the-rack, not 'too traditional-bride'."* (anti-bride streak; perfection expected — *"đưa qua bên chị may lỗi một cái là la làng lên liền… fine dining behave khác."*)
- **Social:** *"Signal taste, status and Vietnamese/Asian-heritage pride; be seen and recognized in my circle."* (viral weddings + "cross credit" from planners/stylists; willing to spend *"mấy chục triệu, cả trăm triệu"*.)

### Trigger (the job only appears with an occasion)
Own wedding · engagement (ăn hỏi) · graduation · an important event worth investing in an outfit. **No occasion → no job** (hence high-ticket, low-frequency).

### Segment variants
- **Việt Kiều (now the largest pool):** *"Reconnect with my Vietnamese/Asian roots through a piece made for my life milestone — even from abroad."* Orders bespoke online + ship; **doesn't need to return to VN** (Michelle corrected the "must come inbound" frame).
- **"Re-wearable" job:** *"Invest in a piece I can wear again across several important events."* → why sườn xám / occasion dress grew to ~50/50 vs áo dài (*"độ ứng dụng rộng hơn; áo dài cưới quá 'cô dâu' rồi khó mặc lại."*).
- **"White-glove certainty" job:** pay designer prices to get it **exactly right**, fully personalized, no mistakes.

### Why this settles the competitor debate
Customers hire Maison for **"designer-made, made-for-a-milestone,"** so they cross-shop **other designer houses** (Công Trí, Huy Võ, Trịnh Hoàng Diệu…), **not** mass brands. This is Chi's point verbatim: *"không có khách nào consider Maison với Xéo Sọ hết."* → the consideration set is the correct competitor-selection criterion (see `competitors.md`).
