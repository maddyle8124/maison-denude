The Atelier

How a piece is made

01

Consultation

An unhurried conversation about occasion, silhouette, and intent. This is where a garment begins.

02

Fabric Selection

Curated textiles — Vietnamese silk, hand-loomed heritage cloth — chosen for the body and the moment.

03

Draping & Pattern

Cut and constructed to your proportions. No standard sizing. Every seam is a decision.

04

Final Fitting

The garment meets its wearer. Adjustments made. Then it belongs to you — entirely.
