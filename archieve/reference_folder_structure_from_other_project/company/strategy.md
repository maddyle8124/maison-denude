# WithDaNang — Company Strategy

_Last updated: 2026-04-13_

## Current Phase

**Pre-launch / Foundation** — building the context system, defining products, planning the main hub MVP.

## Current Focus

Build withdanang.com main hub first. Establish SEO authority and organic traffic before expanding to subdomains.

## Key Bets

1. **Local authenticity as moat** — no algorithm or foreign-run competitor can replicate 23 years of genuine local knowledge and relationships.
2. **SEO automation + human curation** — automated content pipeline to capture Vietnamese-language information, human curation to convert it into expat-relevant content.
3. **Subdomain ecosystem** — once main hub has traffic, subdomains inherit authority and serve high-intent niches (accommodation, experiences, community).
4. **Affiliate-first monetization** — low friction to start, scales with traffic before introducing ads.

## Launch Sequence

1. **withdanang.com main** — events, places, city guide. Builds SEO base and trust.
2. **stay.withdanang.com** — highest affiliate revenue potential.
3. **escape.withdanang.com** — lowest content-production cost (as a local, this is natural).
4. **startup.withdanang.com** — community play, slower burn.
5. **food product (name TBD)** — food is always high traffic, brand to be defined.

## Competitive Positioning

Against: generic travel blogs, Airbnb/Booking, tourist-trap info sites.
Advantage: depth, local trust, expat-specific framing (not tourist framing), longer content for long-tail SEO.

## Growth Model

1. Organic SEO → initial traffic
2. Affiliate revenue → funds content production
3. Community (startup subdomain) → brand moat and repeat engagement
4. Ad placement → layered on top once traffic justifies
