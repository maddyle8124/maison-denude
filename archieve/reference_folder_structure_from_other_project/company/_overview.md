# WithDaNang — Company Overview

_Last updated: 2026-04-13_

## Identity

**Brand name:** WithDaNang
**Domain:** withdanang.com (owned)
**Location:** Da Nang, Vietnam
**Stage:** Pre-launch / Foundation

## Mission

Give non-Vietnamese speakers organic, trustworthy, and locally authentic information about life in Da Nang — cutting through the manipulation of tourist-oriented information systems.

## Vision

A web ecosystem where every expat, digital nomad, and long-stay visitor in Da Nang can find real, unbiased information about where to live, what to do, where to eat, and how to build a life here — sourced from someone who has lived here for 23 years.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** Expats living in Da Nang (1+ month stays, non-Vietnamese speakers)
- **Secondary:** Digital nomads researching Da Nang before arrival
- **Tertiary:** Long-stay tourists wanting to go beyond the tourist bubble
