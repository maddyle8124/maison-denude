# Main Hub — Pain Points, JTBD & Buying Triggers

_Last updated: 2026-04-13_

## Core Pains

| Pain | Description | Affected Personas |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Information is in Vietnamese | Most local listings, community boards, landlord ads, event pages are in Vietnamese — inaccessible to non-speakers | All |
| Tourism manipulation | Tourist-targeted sites inflate prices, hide real options, and push commission-heavy services over genuine recommendations | All |
| Can't find long-term rentals | Airbnb/Booking only shows short-stay options; long-term apartments require Vietnamese-language navigation | Settled Expat, Arriving Nomad |
| No expat-specific city guide | Existing guides are tourist guides (3-day itineraries, photo spots) — not life guides for people staying 3-12 months | Settled Expat |
| Community is scattered | The Da Nang expat community exists in fragmented Facebook groups — no central hub | All |
| Hidden gems are hidden | Local favorite spots, real food experiences, non-tourist areas are only discoverable through local connections | All |

## Buying Triggers

- Arriving in Da Nang for the first time (nomad research phase)
- Signing a long-term lease (triggers service and daily-life research)
- Planning a move to Vietnam (triggers city comparison research)
- Getting scammed or overcharged once (triggers search for trustworthy local source)
- Seeing a Facebook group recommendation for the site

## Purchase Objections (for affiliate products)

- "Is this just another tourist trap referral site?"
- "Will the accommodation be as described?"
- "I don't know if I can trust this source"

**Counter:** Founder credibility (23 years local, former tour guide), transparent affiliate disclosure, no paid-placement masquerading as recommendations.

## Success Criteria (user perspective)

- "I found a real apartment, not Airbnb pricing"
- "I discovered a restaurant that actual locals go to"
- "I made it through my first month without getting ripped off"
- "I found the Da Nang community I was looking for"
