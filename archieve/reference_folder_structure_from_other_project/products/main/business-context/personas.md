# Main Hub — Personas

_Last updated: 2026-04-13_

## Persona Map

| Persona | Description | Priority |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| The Settled Expat | Moved to Da Nang for work or lifestyle, 25-45, living here 3+ months | Primary |
| The Arriving Nomad | Digital nomad, planning 1-3 month stay, researching before arrival | Secondary |
| The Business Builder | Startup founder, remote worker, entrepreneur; seeks community + services | Tertiary |

---

## Primary: The Settled Expat

**Who they are:** Foreign professional or lifestyle expat who has chosen Da Nang as a base. English-speaking, non-Vietnamese. Working remotely, retired, or teaching English. Age 25-45. Already in Da Nang or committed to moving.

**What they need:**
- Reliable local information not filtered through tourist lenses
- Help navigating systems that are in Vietnamese (apartments, services, utilities)
- Community — other expats in similar situations
- Trusted recommendations for daily life (food, services, transport)

**What they don't need:** Tourist-level surface content, trip itineraries for 3-day visits

**Where they hang out:** Facebook groups (Da Nang Expats, etc.), Reddit (r/digitalnomad), word of mouth

---

## Secondary: The Arriving Nomad

**Who they are:** Digital nomad deciding whether to visit Da Nang and for how long. Has budget flexibility. Will stay 1-3 months if the destination earns it. Researching heavily before booking.

**What they need:**
- Honest cost-of-living breakdown
- Best neighborhoods to stay in (not tourist streets)
- Co-working space quality
- Internet reliability, safety, expat community vibe

**High intent for affiliate:** accommodation, long-term apartment rentals, co-working memberships

---

## Tertiary: The Business Builder

**Who they are:** Startup founder, developer, or remote worker who sees Da Nang as a base for building something. Drawn to the city's low cost, growing tech scene, and quality of life.

**What they need:**
- Community of like-minded builders
- Information about legal setup (company registration, visas)
- Networking events and meetups
- Reliable infrastructure info (power, internet, spaces)

**High intent for:** startup.withdanang.com content and community
