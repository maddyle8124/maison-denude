## Competitive Position Notes

No direct competitor owns the "long-stay expat in Da Nang" search space with local-first, SEO-optimized, English content. The closest competition is generic expat sites that cover all of Vietnam or all of Southeast Asia — none go deep on Da Nang specifically from a local perspective.

The moat: a 23-year local who knows the city the way no foreign-run travel blog can.
