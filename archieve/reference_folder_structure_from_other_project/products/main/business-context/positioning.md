# Main Hub — Positioning

_Last updated: 2026-04-13_

## North Star Messaging

**"Da Nang, with locals."**

The resource that non-Vietnamese speakers living in (or moving to) Da Nang use to navigate the city like a local — not like a tourist.

## Core Differentiators

1. **Local authenticity** — written by a Da Nang native with 23 years of lived experience, not a foreign travel blogger passing through
2. **Long-stay focus** — everything is written for people staying 1 month or more, not 3-day tourists
3. **No manipulation** — no paid placements, no tourist traps; affiliate relationships are transparent
4. **Vietnamese → English translation layer** — surfaces locally-known places, events, and services that never make it to English-language sites

## SEO Strategy

Target: long-tail expat-intent queries in English around Da Nang
- "long term apartment rent Da Nang expat"
- "best neighborhoods to live Da Nang foreigner"
- "Da Nang expat community"
- "hidden places Da Nang local"
- "cost of living Da Nang 2026"

Avoid competing on: "things to do in Da Nang", "Da Nang travel guide" — too competitive, wrong intent

## Tone

Honest. Direct. Like a local friend giving advice, not a travel brochure.

## Product Scope

- What's in: daily life information, accommodation, local experiences, community, food, startups — all framed for long-stay non-Vietnamese speakers
- What's out: tourist itineraries, visa processing services (for now), language learning
