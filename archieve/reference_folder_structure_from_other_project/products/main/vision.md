# Main Hub — Vision

_Last updated: 2026-04-19_

## The Problem

Da Nang's information ecosystem for non-Vietnamese speakers is broken. Almost everything that ranks on Google is written by tourists who visited for 5 days, produced by affiliate farms with no local knowledge, or locked inside Vietnamese-language sources. Someone planning a 3-6 month stay has to piece together information from 10-year-old blog posts, generic Booking.com listings, and scattered Facebook groups. They arrive underprepared, get overcharged, and find the community by accident.

## What This Product Is

**withdanang.com is the navigation layer for non-Vietnamese speakers who have chosen Da Nang.**

The key word is *chosen*. This is not a discovery product ("should I go to Da Nang?"). It's a navigation product ("I'm going — help me actually live here"). That distinction drives every content and design decision.

It is:
- A city guide written from 23 years of living here, not 5 days of visiting
- A directory of real local people who can help directly
- An accommodation concierge run by someone who knows landlords personally
- A community hub connecting expats to each other and to the city

It is not:
- A travel blog with affiliate links disguised as recommendations
- A tourist itinerary site (3-day plans, photo spots, Instagram beaches)
- A generic Southeast Asia nomad guide that could be about any city

## Long-Term Vision

withdanang.com becomes the definitive trusted resource for non-Vietnamese speakers living in or relocating to Da Nang. Not a tourist guide — a life guide. The platform expats bookmark, share, and return to weekly.

**And eventually:** a hub where Vietnamese local freelancers, tour guides, and service providers get exposure to an international audience — a marketplace for local talent serving the expat community.

## Core Values

- **Authentic over polished** — real local knowledge beats curated tourist content
- **Expat-first framing** — every piece of content is written for someone planning to stay, not pass through
- **No manipulation** — no paid placements disguised as recommendations; affiliate relationships are disclosed
- **Local voice** — powered by people who actually live here, including the founder's 23-year presence
- **Specific, not vague** — real street names, real prices, real context. Not "a charming local market" but "Con Market on Hung Vuong — go Tuesday morning."

## Brand Voice

The site speaks like a knowledgeable local friend, not a travel brand.
- Direct and specific (real numbers, real names, real streets)
- Honest about downsides (this is what earns trust)
- No hype ("AMAZING", "INCREDIBLE", "hidden gem" are banned)
- Local POV, not tourist POV

## What Success Looks Like

**At MVP launch:**
- 11 pages live, no placeholder content
- First room inquiry submitted via /where-to-stay
- Full SEO audit passed
- Shared organically in a Da Nang expat Facebook group

**3 months:**
- First page rankings on 2-3 long-tail keywords
- First room booking commission earned

**6 months:**
- 1,000+ monthly organic visitors
- 10+ blog posts published
- /communities actively referenced by expat community

**12 months:**
- Recognized brand in the Da Nang expat community (Facebook, Reddit, word of mouth)
- Affiliate revenue covering hosting + content production costs
- /people page growing as more local contributors join
