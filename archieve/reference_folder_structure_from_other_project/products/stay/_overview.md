# Stay — Overview

_Last updated: 2026-04-13_

## Quick Status

**Phase:** Planned — not yet started
**Domain:** stay.withdanang.com
**Priority:** #2 after main hub

## What This Is

Accommodation resource for expats and long-stay visitors in Da Nang. Long-term apartment rentals, serviced apartments, co-living spaces — all framed for non-Vietnamese speakers.

**Revenue model:** Affiliate commissions on accommodation bookings/referrals.

## Why This Exists

Airbnb and Booking.com serve short-stay tourists. Expats and long-stay visitors can't find real long-term rental options in English. Landlords advertise only in Vietnamese. WithDaNang fills that gap.

All product files are placeholders. This product activates after withdanang.com main hub has initial traffic.
