## What This Is

Community hub for entrepreneurs, developers, and remote workers building from Da Nang. Events, meetups, resources for starting a business in Vietnam, and a network of people who chose Da Nang as their base for changing the world.

**Revenue model:** Community events, partner sponsorships, job board referrals (TBD).

## Why This Exists

Da Nang has a growing tech and startup community that is largely invisible online. Foreign builders who arrive don't know the community exists. This connects them with local and expat builders who are already here.

## Founder's Personal Connection

The founder is part of this community — a local tech person who has been building here for years. The authenticity of this product is personal.
