# withdanang.com — Product Vision

_Last updated: 2026-04-19 | Status: DRAFT — to be refined with team_

---

## The Problem We're Solving

Da Nang is one of the most livable cities in Southeast Asia for non-Vietnamese speakers. But the information ecosystem around it is broken in a specific way: almost everything that ranks on Google is either written by tourists who visited for 5 days, produced by affiliate farms with no local knowledge, or locked inside Vietnamese-language sources that expats can't access.

The result: someone planning to move to Da Nang for 3-6 months has to piece together information from 10-year-old blog posts, generic Booking.com listings, and scattered Facebook group threads. They arrive underprepared, get overcharged, and find the community by accident if at all.

**withdanang.com exists to fix that.**

---

## What This Product Is

**withdanang.com is the navigation layer for non-Vietnamese speakers who have chosen Da Nang.**

The key word is *chosen*. This is not a discovery product ("should I go to Da Nang?"). It's a navigation product ("I'm going — help me actually live here"). That distinction drives every content and design decision.

It is:
- A city guide written from 23 years of living here, not 5 days of visiting
- A directory of real local people who can help directly
- An accommodation concierge run by someone who knows landlords personally
- A community hub connecting expats to each other and to the city

It is not:
- A travel blog with affiliate links disguised as recommendations
- A tourist itinerary site (3-day plans, photo spots, best beaches for Instagram)
- A generic Southeast Asia nomad guide that could be about any city

## The User and Their Journey

**Primary user:** The Arriving Nomad or Settled Expat — non-Vietnamese speaker, planning 1-6+ month stay, research-heavy, English-speaking, 25-45.

**Their journey with withdanang.com:**

```
1. Discovery
   Google: "Da Nang cost of living 2026" or "best neighborhoods Da Nang expats"
   → Lands on a cornerstone page
   → First impression: real numbers, real street names, local perspective
   → Trust established in the first 30 seconds

2. Exploration
   Browses from that page → neighborhoods → where to stay → people
   → Builds a mental model of the city before arriving
   → Bookmarks the site

3. Action
   Fills the room inquiry form on /where-to-stay
   → Thieu emails a curated list of real apartments
   → Booking happens outside the site (WhatsApp/direct)
   → User is now a converted lead and a satisfied first contact

4. Retention
   Returns weekly for events, blog posts, community updates
   → Joins a community group found on /communities
   → Connects with a local guide on /people for a tour or service
   → Becomes a word-of-mouth referral source in Da Nang Facebook groups
```

**The moment that defines the product:** A user arrives, finds real information they couldn't find anywhere else, contacts a real local person, and thinks "this is exactly what I needed." That moment is the product.

## Brand Voice

**The site speaks like a knowledgeable local friend — not a travel brand.**

| Quality | What it sounds like |
|---|---|
| Direct | "An Thuong is the expat bubble. High density, high prices. Great if you want to find other nomads easily. Not great if you want to feel like you live in Vietnam." |
| Specific | "Rent in An Thuong runs $400–600/month for a furnished 1BR. Add $40–60 for electricity in summer." |
| Honest about downsides | "Internet in Da Nang is generally reliable, but power outages happen 2-3 times a year during typhoon season." |
| No hype | Never: "Da Nang is an AMAZING city with INCREDIBLE food!" Always: "Da Nang is one of the most practical cities in SEA for remote workers — affordable, fast internet, walkable beach." |
| Local POV | References real street names, real market stalls, real local context. Not "a charming local market" but "Con Market on Hung Vuong — go Tuesday morning before it gets crowded." |

## Design Direction

The visual language must earn trust immediately. The biggest risk for a new site is looking like every other affiliate blog.

**Principles:**
- **Content-first** — information is the product. Design serves it, doesn't compete with it.
- **Real photography** — Da Nang streets, real food, real people. Zero stock photos.
- **Typography-led** — strong font hierarchy, long-form content is comfortable to read
- **Warm but not tropical-tourist-colorful** — not orange-and-turquoise like every Vietnam travel site. Something more grounded and trustworthy.
- **Mobile-first** — most initial discovery happens on a phone

**What to avoid in Stitch:**
- Hero sections with dramatic sunset photos of the Da Nang bridge (every travel site does this)
- Stock photos of smiling tourists
- Neon colors or overly "tropical" palettes
- Cluttered layouts with too many CTAs per page

## MVP Success Definition

The MVP is complete when:

1. A person can Google "Da Nang cost of living 2026" and find withdanang.com in the first page of results within 3 months of launch
2. The first room inquiry arrives via the /where-to-stay form
3. A stranger discovers the site and shares it in a Da Nang expat Facebook group unprompted
4. All 11 pages are live with real, non-placeholder content
5. The site passes the full SEO audit in `seo-foundation.md`

**What MVP is NOT:** Perfect. Beautiful. Feature-complete. It is: live, real, trustworthy, and crawlable.
