## 1. Where We Are Now

Maison Denude has built real cultural capital in Vietnam's premium fashion space — worn by Chi Pu at Vạn Xuân Awards, Băng Di at WeChoice, Văn Mai Hương's MV, and featured in L'Officiel Vietnam, ELLE Vietnam, and Marie Claire. The brand has organic reach across showbiz, editorial, and bridal segments.

The gap: this earned presence isn't being captured or amplified at scale. There's no structured data infrastructure, no keyword-anchored content, and no systematic understanding of which international markets are actually ready for Maison Denude.

**The path we're building toward:**

```
Digital Presence Foundation  →  Global Market Intelligence  →  Digital Marketing Execution  →  Record Global Traction  →  Expand Market Globally
```

## 2. Scope of Work

### 2.1 SEO & Digital Infrastructure — Thiệu

**Tracking Setup (Foundation Layer)**

All subsequent work depends on this being done first. No data = no decisions.

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Google Tag Manager | Central tag management; enables all tracking below |
| Google Analytics 4 | Traffic source analysis, geo data, user behavior |
| Google Search Console | Keyword rankings, crawl health, search visibility |
| Google Business Profile | Local discovery on Google Maps, brand reputation |

**Backlink Building**

Establish initial domain authority through targeted link acquisition — editorial, fashion directories, Vietnamese press that's already covering the brand.

**Entity Claiming**

Identify all existing third-party mentions of Maison Denude (press, directories, aggregators) and claim or associate them with the official website to consolidate authority signals.

**Keyword Strategy**

Target keywords anchored to brand positioning and international discoverability:

- `bespoke saigon` / `bespoke ho chi minh`
- `artisanal bespoke fashion`
- `occasional wear vietnam`
- `heritage inspired dress`
- `asian inspired dress`
- `bespoke ao dai`

**Content: 10 SEO-optimized blog posts** structured around target keywords, brand story, and occasion-based search intent (bridal, red carpet, cultural events).

---

### 2.2 Global Market Research & Execution Plan — Maddy

**Phase 1: Broad Market Scan**

Identify which international markets have demonstrated demand for premium bespoke and fashion design services — prioritizing markets where Maison Denude's positioning (heritage-inspired, artisanal, Vietnamese luxury) has a differentiated angle.

Criteria: disposable income for luxury fashion, cultural openness to Southeast Asian design, existing diaspora or tourism connections to Vietnam.

**Phase 2: 5-Market Deep Dives**

Align with Maison Denude team on 5 priority markets (candidates: Dubai, South Korea, China, Singapore, Japan). For each market, deliver:

| Component | What It Answers |
|-----------|----------------|
| Market Size & Segments | Who's buying, at what price point |
| Competitive Landscape | Who's already in the space, what's the gap |
| Entry Barriers | Distribution, regulation, cultural fit |
| SWOT vs. Maison Denude | Where the brand wins and where it needs work |
| BCG Framework | Portfolio position — star, question mark, etc. |
| Recommended Tactics | How to reach the target segment in that market |

**Phase 3: Execution Plan**

Bridge research to action. The execution plan synthesizes:
- Insights from the 5-market analysis
- Existing brand assets (social content, celebrity associations, editorial features)
- SEO infrastructure being built by Thiệu

Outputs a channel-specific playbook: what to run, where, how to measure it, and in what sequence.
